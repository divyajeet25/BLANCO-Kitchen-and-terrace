Our story

A little Aegean, right above Bhubaneswar

Blanco Kitchen & Terrace is a Santorini-inspired rooftop in Saheed Nagar, built around one idea: an evening that starts with dinner and carries on into the night.

The kitchen cooks across Indian, Italian and Pan-Asian styles, with pasta, grills and noodles made for sharing. The bar pours signature cocktails, and the music grows louder as the sky grows darker.
